CUISINE
Welcome to Seashore

Your Coastal Culinary
Escape

At Seashore Restaurant, we believe that dining is not just about food, but about creating an experience. Nestled along Sorrento's scenic shoreline on the Trans-Canada Highway, our restaurant offers breathtaking views, exceptional service, and a diverse menu that promises to delight your taste buds.

Reimagined by Executive Chef Ravi, with over 20 years of culinary experience spanning Canada and international cruise ships — we've transformed this space into Sorrento's most welcoming dining destination.
